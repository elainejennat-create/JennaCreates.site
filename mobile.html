here's a little about me

Hi, I’m Jenna — Virtual Assistant by day, digital marketing creative by default.

I started in customer service, sales, and marketing support... and somewhere along the way realized I actually love turning other people’s messy ideas into something organized and engaging.

Picked up social media management, content creation, graphic design, short-form video editing, copywriting, SEO, and marketing systems along the way — basically a buffet, and I ate it all.

creative + strategic

I gravitate toward the creative and strategic side of digital marketing, but I secretly love the behind-the-scenes chaos too — taking a messy project and making it run smooth.

I’m always hunting for a new tool or a faster way to do things, because why make it harder than it needs to be?

my goal:

Grow into a well-rounded digital marketing pro, working with people and brands who actually value creativity, organization, and work that isn’t rushed.
